# References

Primary citations used in the OLLMU concept paper. Items are grouped by role. This list is not a claim that any cited work implements OLLMU.

DOI targets below were resolved against the Crossref API on 2026-09-07 (HTTP 200). Prefer `https://doi.org/…` as the stable link.

## Slow light, Kerr, reservoir / delay (literature)

7. T. Baba, “Slow light in photonic crystals,” *Nature Photonics* **2**, 465–473 (2008). DOI: [10.1038/nphoton.2008.146](https://doi.org/10.1038/nphoton.2008.146).

8. R. W. Boyd, *Nonlinear Optics*, 4th ed., Academic Press (2020). Textbook treatment of the optical Kerr effect, \(n = n_0 + n_2 I\). No DOI; cite the book.

9. R. Liu, Z. Wang, C. Zhong, et al., “Femto-joule threshold reconfigurable all-optical nonlinear activators for picosecond pulsed optical neural networks,” *Light: Science & Applications* **15**, 128 (2026). 11. G. Van der Sande, D. Brunner, M. C. Soriano, “Advances in photonic reservoir computing,” *Nanophotonics* **6**, 561–576 (2017). DOI: [10.1515/nanoph-2016-0132](https://doi.org/10.1515/nanoph-2016-0132).

12. I. Boikov, D. Brunner, A. De Rossi, “Direct coupling of nonlinear integrated cavities for all-optical reservoir computing,” arXiv:2307.10950 (2023). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.10950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.10950).

## Visible-wavelength platforms (literature)

13. M. A. Green, “Self-consistent optical parameters of intrinsic silicon at 300 K including temperature coefficients,” *Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells* **92**, 1305–1310 (2008). DOI: [10.1016/j.solmat.2008.06.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solmat.2008.06.009). Silicon absorbs through the visible; ~450 nm waveguides are not SOI silicon cores.

14. N. Chauhan, J. Wang, D. Bose, R. Moreira, D. J. Blumenthal, “Ultra-Low Loss 698 nm and 450 nm Silicon Nitride Visible Wavelength Waveguides for Strontium Atomic Clock Applications,” *CLEO* (2020). DOI: [10.1364/CLEO_SI.2020.STh1J.2](https://doi.org/10.1364/CLEO_SI.2020.STh1J.2). Reported ~0.08 dB/cm at 450 nm (conference abstract).

15. R. Arefin, S. H. Ramachandra, H. Jung, W. You, S. M. N. Hasan, H. Turski, S. Dwivedi, S. Arafin, “III-N/Si₃N₄ Integrated Photonics Platform for Blue Wavelengths,” *IEEE Journal of Quantum Electronics* **56** (2020). DOI: [10.1109/JQE.2020.2993634](https://doi.org/10.1109/JQE.2020.2993634). Theoretical coupling study (not a fabricated BIC array).

16. X. Mu, F. Weiss, H. Chua, et al., “Hybrid Integration of InGaN Lasers in a Foundry-Fabricated Visible-Light Photonics Platform,” *Advanced Photonics Research* (2026). DOI: [10.1002/nap2.70037](https://doi.org/10.1002/nap2.70037). Preprint: [arXiv:2510.16699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.16699). Experimental flip-chip bonding of 450 nm InGaN diodes onto SiN.

## Weight movement (literature and industrial context)

22. M. Horowitz, “Computing's energy problem (and what we can do about it),” *IEEE International Solid-State Circuits Conference Digest of Technical Papers*, 10–14 (2014). DOI: [10.1109/ISSCC.2014.6757323](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISSCC.2014.6757323). Plenary text: a DRAM access is of order 1–2 nJ; DRAM I/O is above 20 pJ/bit. The circulated 45 nm table (Fig. 1.1.9) lists a 32-bit DRAM read at 640 pJ and an 8-bit multiply at 0.2 pJ. Teaching anchor from 2014, not a 2026 HBM measurement. Added to this list 2026-10-21.

23. Taalas, “The path to ubiquitous AI.” Company essay: [https://taalas.com/the-path-to-ubiquitous-ai/](https://taalas.com/the-path-to-ubiquitous-ai/) (checked 2026-10-21). Industrial context only: a model hard-wired into a chip so inference does not haul weights from off-chip memory; Llama 3.1 8B; SRAM kept for KV-cache and LoRA; of order two months from a received model to hardware. Not a photonic device and not evidence that OLLMU exists.
